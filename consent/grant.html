Produce an operator grant

A grant is one sentence your origin publishes: this account may spend under these limits. It is the permission that authority_receipt.py already checks and that nothing, until now, produced.

This page holds no key, signs nothing, and sends nothing anywhere. It writes a sentence your origin publishes. There is no network request of any kind on this page, no external script, no font and no analytics — read the source and confirm it. Producing a grant does not move money and cannot move money.

What this buys, and what it does not

It makes your policy citable and stranger-checkable: anyone holding a receipt can fetch your grant, pin it by digest, and see for themselves whether the payment was inside the limits you published. It does not make you trustworthy, it does not hold or move funds, and it is not a signature — the Nano network already verified who signed the block. This is the separate question of why the act was permitted.

Revocation is a bump: raise policy_epoch and serve the new bytes. Every receipt citing the old epoch stops verifying the moment you do. Nano cannot un-send a block, so this is the only revocation the ledger permits — which is why the permission is fetched live instead of being embedded in the receipt.
